Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tile Stack collects, uses, and protects your information when you play the game on our website. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Tile Stack is designed to minimize data collection. The only information stored is:

  • Local game progress: Your current level, score, and settings are saved in your browser's local storage so you can continue playing later.
  • Usage data from ads: We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google may collect certain information through cookies and similar technologies as described in the Google Privacy & Terms.

We do not collect names, emails, addresses, or any other personally identifiable information directly.

How We Use Information

  • To save your game progress locally in your browser.
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense.
  • To understand aggregated traffic patterns and improve the site experience.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Data Security

Local game data is stored only on your device. We do not transmit or store it on our servers. While we aim to keep the site secure, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Tile Stack is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Please review it periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the About page.
