Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Tile Stack gameplay, features, privacy, and more.

Is Tile Stack free to play?

Yes. Tile Stack is completely free to play. You do not need to download an app, create an account, or pay anything. Open the game in your browser and start matching tiles.

Does Tile Stack work on mobile?

Yes. The game is built with a responsive layout and touch-friendly controls. It works on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, as well as desktop browsers like Chrome, Safari, Edge, and Firefox.

How do I save my progress?

Your progress is saved automatically in your browser's local storage. As long as you return on the same device and browser, you can continue from where you left off. Clearing browser data will reset your progress.

What are Undo and Shuffle?

Undo lets you reverse your last move, which is helpful when you accidentally tap the wrong tile. Shuffle rearranges the remaining tiles on the board, giving you new matching opportunities when you are stuck.

How many slots do I have?

You have seven slots. If all seven fill up and no three tiles can be matched, the level ends. The key to winning is keeping your slots clear and balanced.

What is the Daily Challenge?

The Daily Challenge is a fixed level that is the same for every player on a given day. Come back each day to compete on a fresh board and compare your progress with friends.

Can I play offline?

Tile Stack is a browser game, so you need an internet connection to load it. Once loaded, the core gameplay can continue without a connection depending on your browser cache.

Are there ads?

We may display ads to help support the game's development. Ads are served by Google AdSense and are clearly labeled. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.

Is my data collected?

Tile Stack stores only local game progress in your browser. We do not require accounts, emails, or personal information. For full details, please read our Privacy Policy.

How can I report a bug or suggest a feature?

We welcome feedback. You can reach out through the contact information on our About page.
